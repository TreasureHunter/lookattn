We evaluate our method and all the baselines on ten image-based benchmarks, which can be divided into two categories: the first category consists of five text- and diagram-oriented VQA tasks, including DocVQA, TextVQA, ChartQA, OCRBench, and AI2D; the second category consists of five general and reasoning VQA tasks, including POPE, MME, ScienceQA-IMG, MMMU, and RealWorldQA. For video-language understanding, we select three benchmarks, including MVBench, VideoMME, and SEEDBench.
